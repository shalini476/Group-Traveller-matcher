Hackathon Problem 3: The 6 Strict Matching Rules

Rule Logic & Constraints Backend Implementation
1. Exact Destination Both travel to the exact same city (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Dubai, Abu Dhabi). Case-insensitive exact string match.
2. Dates Overlap Group and seeker share at least 2 nights in destination. Calculated via date window intersection: shared_nights >= 2. Dynamic sliding scale: ≥95% (✓ Green), 50–94% (⚠ Yellow), <50% (✗ Red).
3. Budget Compatibility Difference between budgets is at most 40% of the lower budget. abs(b1 - b2) <= 0.40 * min(b1, b2). Closeness scale: ≥95% (✓ Green), 50–94% (⚠ Yellow), <50% (✗ Red).
4. Shared Interests Seeker shares at least 2 interests with the group owner. len(seeker_interests & owner_interests) >= 2.
5. Gender Preference Mutual compatibility: seeker allows owner AND owner allows seeker. Evaluates SAME_GENDER vs ANY mutually both ways.
6. Group Size Current member count + seeker does not exceed group's max_size. len(member_ids) + 1 <= max_size.
Feature 4 – Auto-Decline Imminent Trips Policy: Join requests for groups whose check_in departure is within 5 days or less of the current date are automatically rejected by the system with a clear status notice.
